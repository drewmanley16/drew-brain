# Skill: Research Company

## Purpose

Research a company for recruiting, interviews, partnerships, investing, or general understanding.

## Process

1. Clarify the reason for the research from context.
2. Find the company's product, customers, business model, and recent direction.
3. Identify the details most relevant to Drew's goal.
4. Separate verified facts from inference.
5. Summarize the useful information instead of dumping everything found.

## Recruiting Focus

When researching for an interview, prioritize:

- product
- engineering challenges
- technical stack when reliable
- current initiatives
- role expectations
- culture signals
- thoughtful questions Drew can ask
