# Skill: Weekly Review

## Purpose

Turn the week's activity into a useful review and next-action plan.

## Process

1. Review calendar, project notes, tasks, and meaningful messages if available.
2. Identify what moved forward.
3. Identify blockers and things that were repeatedly postponed.
4. Capture meaningful decisions or lessons into memory.
5. Pick the most important priorities for the next week.
6. Identify work that could be automated or converted into a skill.

## Output

### What moved

### What got stuck

### Decisions and lessons

### Next week

### Candidates for automation
