# Skill: IG Personal Story / Series Post

## Purpose
Multi-part personal narrative content ("part 1 of what I'd do if I was a
freshman"). Builds a follow-driven series instead of a one-off value post.

## Before writing
Read `context/content-instagram.md` for voice rules.

## Inputs to identify
- The series topic and which part this is
- The personal credibility detail (specific, self-deprecating > generic
  authority — e.g. "3.4 gpa in high school who landed an offer")
- The actual advice/step for this part
- What's being teased for next part

## Structure
1. Hook: a relatable claim about what people underestimate/get wrong.
2. State this is part [N] of a series.
3. Ground it with a specific, humble personal credibility line.
4. Deliver the actual advice/step for this part — concrete, not vague
   ("ditch the high school template, use jakes resume on overleaf" not
   "make your resume better").
5. Optional CTA for a resource related to this part specifically.
6. Close with a forward tease + "follow for part [N+1]" — not a comment CTA.

## Output
Plain lines, no markdown formatting.
