# Skill: Respond to Brand Email

## Purpose

Evaluate an inbound brand or sponsorship email and draft the appropriate response.

## Process

1. Identify the company and product.
2. Extract what they are asking for.
3. Extract deliverables, timeline, usage rights, exclusivity, and compensation when present.
4. Evaluate audience fit.
5. Determine whether Drew should accept, negotiate, ask for more information, or decline.
6. Draft a concise response in Drew's voice.

## Negotiation Priorities

Consider:

- effort required
- number of deliverables
- content usage rights
- exclusivity
- turnaround time
- audience fit
- whether the company wants raw UGC or content posted to Drew's account

## Safety Rule

Never send a message or agree to commercial terms without Drew's approval unless he explicitly delegates that authority.
