# Skill: Write an Email Reply

## Purpose
Draft a reply to an email in Drew's actual voice — brand negotiations, fan/
advice questions, quick logistics, or polite declines.

## Before writing
Read `context/content-email.md` for voice rules and situation-specific
patterns.

## Inputs to identify
- Who's emailing and what they actually asked/said
- Which situation this is: cold pitch, brand negotiation, fan/advice,
  quick logistics, or decline acknowledgment (see context file — each has a
  different length and tone)
- Any concrete terms to state (rate, scope, dates)
- Whether to leave the door open for future contact

## Process
1. Identify the situation type first — it determines length and warmth
   more than anything else.
2. Open with "Hi/Hey [Name]," then a genuine thanks/acknowledgment line.
3. Answer directly. Match paragraph length to the situation (quick
   logistics = 1-2 sentences, fan replies = warmest and most detailed).
4. State any terms/asks plainly and confidently, no hedging.
5. Leave a door open if there's future potential, only if genuine.
6. Sign off "Best,\nDrew" (default) or "Best regards, Drew Manley" (formal/
   distant, e.g. declines).

## Output
Plain email text, ready to send, full sentences and normal punctuation.
