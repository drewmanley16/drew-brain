# Skill: IG Internship News Post

## Purpose
Time-sensitive post about newly opened internship applications. E.g. the
"Summer 2027 internship" scripts.

## Before writing
Read `context/content-instagram.md` for voice rules.

## Inputs to identify
- Target grad year / internship cycle (e.g. Summer 2027)
- Companies that just opened roles (name real specific ones, 4-6)
- The urgency reason (rolling review, applications flood fast, etc.)
- Whether Drew has a resource (repo/site) to point to for finding these
- CTA word

## Sourcing companies
Pull today's real openings from Drew's daily swelist.com email (Gmail, sender
`noreply@swelist.com`, subject like "X New Internships Posted Today") rather
than guessing or using stale examples. Pick 4-6 names recognizable to a
college CS/tech audience (skip niche/irrelevant roles like geomatics or
survey internships even if they're in the email). swelist.com itself is
usually the resource to point to in the CTA.

## Structure
1. Hook: direct address to the audience + why today matters ("if you're
   looking for a [cycle] internship, today might be the most important day
   of the season").
2. Name the companies that just dropped roles — the names carry the weight,
   don't over-adjective this line.
3. Urgency line: why applying now vs. later matters (rolling review, high
   volume).
4. Optional: tease the method/site Drew uses to catch postings early.
5. CTA: "comment [WORD] and I'll send you the repo/link with all the
   applications."

## Notes
Drew typically drafts 2-3 hook variations for this format before picking
one — the winning version leads with stakes, not adjectives. When drafting,
give 2 hook options and let him pick rather than committing to one.

## Output
Plain lines, no markdown formatting.
