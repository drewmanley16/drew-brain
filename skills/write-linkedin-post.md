# Skill: Write LinkedIn Post

## Purpose

Turn an experience, observation, project, or career lesson into a LinkedIn post that still sounds like Drew.

## Process

1. Start with what actually happened.
2. Give enough context for the reader to understand why it mattered.
3. State the takeaway in plain language.
4. Use specific details when available.
5. Stop before the post becomes motivational filler.

## Rules

- Do not sound like a corporate creator.
- Avoid generic gratitude paragraphs.
- Avoid "I am thrilled to announce" unless Drew explicitly wants that tone.
- Avoid exaggerated life lessons.
- Keep Drew's humor when relevant.
- Do not use em dashes.
