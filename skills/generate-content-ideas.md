# Skill: Generate Content Ideas

## Purpose

Generate content ideas that fit Drew's audience and existing style.

## Process

1. Look at what Drew is currently learning, building, applying to, or experiencing.
2. Find the useful or surprising angle.
3. Prefer ideas with a clear promise to the viewer.
4. Avoid repeating recent posts unless there is a meaningfully different angle.
5. Favor ideas that can be demonstrated with examples, screenshots, code, resources, or a real story.

## Good Categories

- technical concepts explained simply
- resources people can immediately use
- internship and recruiting lessons
- behind-the-scenes building
- experiments and challenges
- reactions to useful industry resources
