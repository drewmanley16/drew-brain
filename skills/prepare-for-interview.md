# Skill: Prepare for Interview

## Purpose

Build focused interview preparation based on the company, role, level, timeline, and Drew's current strengths and weaknesses.

## Process

1. Identify interview format and likely areas tested.
2. Separate preparation into coding, system design, behavioral, and role-specific topics as relevant.
3. Prioritize the highest-probability material first.
4. Create a study plan based on the available time.
5. Use active practice rather than passive reading whenever possible.
6. Track weak areas for follow-up.

## Coding Interview Rule

When solving a problem interactively, guide Drew through the reasoning before giving the optimal solution unless he asks for the answer directly.
