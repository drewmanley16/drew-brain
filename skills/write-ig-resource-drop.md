# Skill: IG Resource Drop

## Purpose
Script for sharing a single resource (repo, tool, site) that solves a
specific problem. E.g. "tech oa by fast prep" script.

## Before writing
Read `context/content-instagram.md` for voice rules.

## Inputs to identify
- The resource (exact name/path)
- The specific problem it solves
- Proof it's legit (who uses it, what's in it, why it's current)
- CTA word

## Structure
1. Hook: short and firm, one clear statement. No hedging ("probably",
   "might be"), no soft framing ("isnt translating to"). State the problem
   as fact in as few words as possible ("the reason you are failing your
   OA's is because you havent used this repo" not "the reason your leetcode
   practice isnt translating to actual interviews is you're probably...").
2. Name the resource immediately.
3. 1-2 lines of specific proof (what's inside, who it covers, why it's
   trustworthy/current).
4. One line on how to use it (e.g. "practice questions for the company
   you're interviewing at first").
5. CTA: "comment [WORD] and I'll send you the link."

## Output
Plain lines, no markdown formatting.
