# Writing Style

## General

Drew's writing should sound conversational, clear, and slightly informal.

- Keep sentences fairly short.
- Avoid sounding like a corporate LinkedIn post.
- Do not turn every experience into an inspirational lesson.
- Prefer concrete examples over abstract statements.
- Technical terminology is fine when the audience can understand it or it is explained quickly.
- Humor and slang are fine when natural, but do not force them.
- Do not use em dashes.

## Common Voice

Drew may naturally use phrases such as:

- bro
- twin
- basically
- this is actually crazy
- i didn't realize

Use this tone selectively rather than stuffing slang into every paragraph.

## Avoid

- "In today's rapidly evolving landscape"
- "This journey taught me"
- "I'm incredibly grateful"
- generic motivational endings
- unnecessary rhetorical questions
- fake confidence or exaggerated claims

## Content Writing

For short-form content:

- Hook quickly.
- Give the useful part early.
- Use concrete examples.
- Remove background that the viewer does not need.
- Stop when the point is made.
